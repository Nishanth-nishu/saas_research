# Market Overview — four hypotheses compared

Research date: 2026-10-28. This is a synthesis. The detailed findings tables, with a source URL and an evidence
label on every claim, are in:
- `research/parts/market_AB.md` — A: education/coaching/study-abroad; B: distributors, forwarders, document processing
- `research/parts/market_CD.md` — C: agencies and B2B services; D: small SaaS and D2C e-commerce

Labels used: **FACT** (seen on an official or primary page), **VENDOR_CLAIM**, **CUSTOMER_OPINION**, **HYPOTHESIS** (our own interpretation).

## 1. Research coverage and limits
- About 80 sources loaded across the two market passes, 15 practitioners were profiled in the acquisition study, and 95 prospect websites were verified.
- **Blocked:** Reddit (all queries), G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, AmbitionBox, Indeed/Naukri company pages, Clutch and GoodFirms. Customer-opinion evidence therefore comes from Trustpilot, the Shopify App Store, SoftwareFinder, mystery-shopping and survey data, and job posts. **This is the largest gap in the research.**
- Many "pain" statistics found are vendor claims. They are labelled as such and were not used as proof.

## 3. Key findings by segment

### A. Coaching institutes and study-abroad consultancies
- **Follow-up is weak even at well-funded institutions.** In a 2025 mystery-shopping study of 103 universities in the UK, ANZ, Canada and the US, only 1 in 4 enquirers received any follow-up. WhatsApp was the most responsive channel. (FACT as reported by ICEF Monitor: https://monitor.icef.com/2025/10/mystery-shopping-study-finds-broad-improvement-in-student-enquiry-handling-this-year/)
- **Education agents' back-office is under-digitised.** 39% have no CRM, 65% invoice manually and track in Excel, and 67% have no commission software. (Survey reported by Flywire; probably about 2022: https://www.flywire.com/resources/education-agents-turn-to-technology-as-demand-for-services-soar)
- **Job posts show the manual work.** Counsellors must log every lead interaction, follow-up note and status change in the CRM, track daily calls and send reminders. (FACT: https://www.erekrut.com/jobs/admissions-counsellor-job-at-intelligent-education-uttar-pradesh-noida-erekrut-job-portal/) Telecallers average ₹16,383/month (https://in.indeed.com/career/telecaller/salaries).
- **Existing spend (budget evidence):**
  - LeadSquared: ₹90k–1.49L/yr (FACT).
  - WhatsApp tools: Interakt ₹2.5–3.8k/mo; Wati ₹2.7–16.8k/mo, with round-robin assignment only on the ₹16.8k Business tier (FACT).
  - Meritto (no price published): reviewers call it "too costly … for smaller organizations" (CUSTOMER_OPINION).
- **Indian agencies publish build prices** of ₹35k–7L per workflow, plus about ₹8k/mo upkeep (VENDOR_CLAIM: codingclave.com, sotlabs.net).

### B. Distributors, forwarders and document processing
- **Purchase bill → Tally is real but commoditised.** A CA published a DIY Python tool on ICAI's site (FACT). TallyPrime 4.0 has native Excel import (FACT). Vyapar TaxOne does unlimited Tally entry for **₹10,000/yr** (FACT). The price anchor is very low.
- **Shipping documents are higher value per document.** Documentation executive pay is about ₹40–60k/mo in Mumbai (snippet-only). Enterprise tools cost more than Indian SMBs pay: Rossum is from **$18,000/yr** (FACT); Raft and Expedock are sold by demo only. The local forwarding software Logi-Sys now advertises AI document reading (VENDOR_CLAIM). That is the main threat.
- **Regulation:** GST e-invoicing is mandatory above ₹5 Cr AATO, and DPDP core obligations apply from about May 2027 (PIB, FACT).

### C. Agencies and B2B services
- **Reporting tools are cheap and all ship AI summaries:** AgencyAnalytics $20/client/mo; DashThis, Swydo and Whatagraph (FACT). Differentiation for custom builds is low.
- **3 agencies have open roles whose duties include client reporting** (Raleigh Digital, GEL Studios and Digital Mojo; the first two re-verified 2026-10-28). The workflow is still staffed by hand in some agencies.
- **RFP/proposal work:** 33 h per RFP (27 h for SMBs) per Loopio's survey (VENDOR_CLAIM). SMB frequency is unknown.

### D. Small SaaS and D2C
- **AI support is priced per resolution and cheap:** Help Scout $0.75, Intercom Fin $0.99, Zendesk $2.00, Gorgias about $0.90–1.00 (FACT). Gorgias's AI already edits and cancels Shopify orders (FACT).
- **Indian support salaries average about ₹2.6L/yr** (Payscale), which caps automation ROI for Indian D2C brands.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** the only visible gap is Indian 3PL and courier integration glue (Shiprocket, Unicommerce, COD/RTO). It is unvalidated.

### Alternatives surfaced (not deep-dived)
- **Indian CA firms (98,967 firms):** document collection and WhatsApp reminders. Deadline-driven pain, but a low price anchor (TaxOne ₹10k/yr).
- **Recruitment CV screening:** weak, because Manatal includes AI scoring from $15/user/mo.
- **Real-estate inquiry handling (India):** vendor-claim evidence only.

## 4. Patterns across segments (HYPOTHESIS)
1. **The software is cheap in every segment.** A small vendor will not win by selling a cheaper tool. It can win by **configuring and connecting** tools the buyer already pays for (WhatsApp API, Sheets, Tally, CRM) around one painful workflow, and by owning the last mile: exceptions, ledger mapping, follow-up discipline.
2. **Published Indian implementation prices (₹35k–₹1L+ per workflow)** leave room for a ₹25–60k pilot. They also show buyers already accept paying for implementation separately from software.
3. **Segments A and B are local, relationship-driven and India-priced.** C and D are global but crowded with funded SaaS. For a solo founder in Hyderabad with a ₹10–30k budget, A and B have better buyer access and less AI-SaaS competition at the SMB end.

## 5. Conflicting evidence
- Enquiry handling is improving year on year (Edified), so the gap is concentrated in follow-up, not first response.
- LeadSquared's own pricing page conflicts with its comparison blog. ExtraaEdge's third-party prices conflict on the same page.
- Vendor claims conflict: "manual BOL entry persists" (imagetotable.ai) versus "93% of documents fully automated" (Raft).
- Reporting time: 2–5 h per report (vendor blogs) versus "under 30 minutes" (AgencyAnalytics).
- WISMO share of tickets: 18%–50% across secondary sources.
