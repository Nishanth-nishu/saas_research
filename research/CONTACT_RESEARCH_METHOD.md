## 2. Contact classification (used in `contact_type`)
1. `verified_public_business_contact` — email/phone printed on the company's own site or an authoritative directory.
2. `public_professional_profile` — person named with role on an official page; or a profile URL seen in a public source.
3. `official_contact_form` — the company's own form/contact page.
4. `third_party_unverified` — from a directory or aggregator, not confirmed on the official site. (None stored.)
5. `unknown` — no legitimate route found yet.

## 3. Who to reach, by segment
| Segment | Workflow | Most relevant role | Fallback route |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Education / study-abroad | Inquiry follow-up, counsellor assignment | Founder/Director; Head of Admissions/Counselling; Operations Manager | Official inquiry email or the branch manager's published number (business hours) |
| B Distribution / logistics | Document data entry, reconciliation | Owner/Director (family businesses); Accounts Head; Operations/Documentation Manager | info@ / admin@ inbox addressed to "Accounts/Operations head" |
| C Agencies | Client reporting | Founder/CEO; Head of Delivery/Account Management; Performance lead | hello@ inbox or founder's LinkedIn (manual) |
| D D2C / SaaS | Support triage, WISMO | Founder; Head of CX/Support; Ops lead | support@ is the wrong inbox for sales — use the press/partnerships/founder route or LinkedIn |

## 4. Step-by-step method for one prospect (≈10–15 min)
1. Open the official site: `/about`, `/team`, `/leadership`, `/contact`, footer. Record a person only if the page states their role.
2. If no person: search `"<company>" founder OR director OR CEO` and accept only results from the company's site, a press release, an MCA/government filing summary, or a news article that names the role. Record the URL.
3. LinkedIn: search manually **from your own account**, view the profile, and paste the URL into `linkedin_url` yourself. No automation or scraping tools.
4. Email: use only addresses printed on the site. If only a form exists, the form is the channel. Do not use pattern guesses or unverified enrichment tools.
5. Record `source` for every field, set `last_verified` to today's date.

## 5. What is deliberately not done
- No scraping of LinkedIn, Google Maps, or job boards behind logins; no CAPTCHA/anti-bot bypass.
- No purchased lists, no paid enrichment (Apollo/Hunter/Lusha) without your approval.
- No personal mobile numbers, home addresses, or personal social accounts.

### Recommended queries by niche (copied from field notes; all tested at least once)
**A — study-abroad / coaching**
- `"study abroad" consultancy Kerala OR Gujarat OR Punjab branches`
- `"overseas education counsellor" job "<city>" weekday OR teamlease`
- `"admission counsellor" OR "tele caller" IELTS OR NEET OR UPSC institute <city> job`
- `"<exam> coaching" "our branches" <state>`

**B — distributors / forwarders**
- `"[city] freight forwarder" OR CHA OR "pharma C&F superstockist" "about us"`
- `"super stockist" OR "C&F agent" [city] Pvt Ltd careers accounts billing`
- `"[company] careers" "data entry operator" distributor [city] Tally`
- `"Customs House Agent" "since 19" [city] Pvt Ltd about us`

**C — agencies**
- `"[city] digital marketing agency" careers "client reporting" "Looker Studio"`
- `"paid media executive" "Looker Studio" agency` (UK, Australia)
- `"SEO reporting specialist" agency careers GA4 "Search Console"`

**D — D2C / SaaS**
- `site:talentmate.com "Customer Support" "Freshdesk" "Shopify"`
- `site:jobs.lever.co OR site:job-boards.greenhouse.io "customer support" India e-commerce`
- `"Shiprocket" "Freshdesk" "customer support executive" job`
- `"NDR" OR "RTO" "customer support" job India D2C`

### Verification rule for any discovered business
Official site loads and names the business, or an authoritative directory (government/association) lists it.
A listicle or job aggregator alone does **not** verify existence.
